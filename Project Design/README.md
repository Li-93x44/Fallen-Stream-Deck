# FALLEN Stream Deck 
The first question people might have when they see the word "Stream Deck" is what is a Stream Deck? 
Well to answer that question we'll have to first look at the people who use it,  ex: Livestreamers, Video editors, Photo editors, Graphic designers, Gamers, sometimes regular people, and colorists even (although the ones they use are solely for colouring and nothing else). From the people I've mentioned it might create another question what do they need it for? DOn't worry I'll answer that as well.

Let's go through each of the above mentioned person's use for a Stream deck. 
1. Livestreamers: Most of the time stream decks are made for live streamers then the others modify it for themselves, Livestreamers use it to control app switching, volume switching, Visuals switching, Mic controls, camera controls, and so on.
2. Gamers: Gamers also use it for similar things as Livestreamers do suh as Volume controls, Mic controls, and if they are livesreaming their uses will include a livestreamer's uses as well.
3. Video editors: video editors Use a stream deck to make use of shortcuts which aren't already available in the keyboard, they also use it to move in the timeline and zoom in and out of the timline.
4. Photo editors: Photo editors would typically use the knobs in a stream deck on the colour wheel and also the the moving bars for a more acurate edit.
5. Graphic Designers: Graphic Designers wiuld use the stream deck in a similar way as to video editor except for seeking ahead on a timmline (graphic design software doesn't have a timeline) 
6. Colorists: Colorists typically have a stream deck like panel built for them and theirr use specifically, most of the time it will be Davinci Resolve focused. Photo ediotrs and Colorsits have a small venn diagram of common things which is the colouring/ color grading wheels
7. The regular joe: They have no use they just bought it and plugged it in becuase it seems "Aesthetic", in reality they have no idea what it is or how to use it.
